Ritornello: From Italian ritornare, to return. A musical theme that recurs periodically during a piece, providing 
structural unity; a refrain. Ritornello is a technical term in concerto writing for the simpler theme played by the
ripieno or tutti, in contrast to the virtuosic writing for the soloists (Greenberg 1998).
The retornello theme returns in segments, never in its entirety.

Exposition: Usually the first part of a piece of music where all the musical ideas are presented

Development: Follows the exposition. This is the secion where the introduced musical ideas are explored.

Recapitulation: Follows the development. This is the section where the musical ideas introduced in the exposition return.
